# Seven-Figure Scorecard

Ava is evaluated on profitable, compliant progress rather than close rate alone.

| Metric                                         |           Target | Weight |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------: | -----: |
| Qualified appointments per 100 contacted leads |       25 or more |    15% |
| Signed and surviving contracts per month       |       10 or more |    20% |
| Average net assignment revenue                 |  $12,000 or more |    20% |
| Cancellation or rescission rate                |         under 5% |    10% |
| Seller complaints and DNC incidents            |                0 |    15% |
| Gross profit per contacted lead                |   $2,000 or more |    10% |
| Time to verbal commitment on qualified calls   | under 10 minutes |     5% |
| Underwriting accuracy                          |       within 10% |     5% |

## RBP metrics

- RBP acceptance rate on qualified high-repair leads: 30% or higher.
- Average seller net improvement versus cash path: 20% or higher where verified.
- RBP closed deals per month.
- RBP fallout rate and reason codes.

## Per-call scoring

Each call should produce:

- Phase progression
- Evidence gaps
- Seller emotion and policy applied
- Offer/path policy applied
- Objections and responses
- Compliance warnings
- Follow-up outcome
- Coaching recommendation
