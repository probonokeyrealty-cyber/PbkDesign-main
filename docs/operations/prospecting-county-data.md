# Prospecting county data

The County Data view uses the [2025 Census Gazetteer](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/time-series/geo/gazetteer-files.2025.html) for county and county-equivalent identity (50 states, DC, and Puerto Rico). The directory is a bundled index, not a registry of approved access. It makes no runtime Census or county calls. To regenerate that version of the index, run `scripts/refresh-prospecting-county-directory.ps1` from the repo.

Los Angeles County is the first source entry. Start with the [county parcel export](https://egis-lacounty.hub.arcgis.com/datasets/parcels) and the [Assessor roll](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=70d93266f45a4080a97b285a471493cd&sublayer=0); confirm intended use under the publisher's terms. To inspect a subset, extract a CSV locally with AIN and Property Location or SitusAddress. Roll Year and use are optional. The preview reads up to 8 MB and 25,000 rows, only in browser memory, and does not verify the file's provenance. Owner and contact columns are not included in the preview.

For other counties, the copied Rex brief is a human-initiated handoff to Brain to find official documentation. It does not launch research or start a run. Before adding a county source, verify the publisher, allowed use, required permissions, field coverage, update cadence, and exact jurisdiction. A published source link does not register an execution adapter or permit scraping. ATTOM, scheduled access, CRM writes, outreach, and promotion are out of scope.
