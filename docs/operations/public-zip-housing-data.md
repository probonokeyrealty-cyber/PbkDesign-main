# Public ZIP housing data

This is staff-only reference context. It does not replace the saved house, the fictional listing comparison, or underwriting inputs. It does not drive contact targeting, qualification, investor visibility, or property valuation. The browser loads a versioned app asset; it does not send a ZIP or any lead data to a provider.

DeepSeek was used to discover candidate datasets; official publishers and actual downloadable files are the source of record. Free downloads are not blanket permission to embed or redistribute data.

## Refresh procedure

Download the two individual tables from the [2024 Census summary-file directory](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/summary_file/2024/table-based-SF/data/5YRData/) as `b25064.dat` and `b25077.dat`; get [the FHFA workbook](https://www.fhfa.gov/hpi/download/annual/hpi_at_zip5.xlsx) as `hpi_at_zip5.xlsx`. Store raw files outside the deploy and do not commit them. This importer uses Python 3.11+ and `openpyxl==3.1.5` for the Excel source only.

```powershell
python scripts/import-public-zip-housing.py .pbk-local/zip-source-inputs
```

The importer checks each source SHA-256, rejects duplicate rows, strips non-ZCTA Census geographies, and omits Census estimates without a numeric, nonnegative published margin of error (including capped medians). It keeps the most recent usable FHFA change in 2024 or 2025, labels the observation year, and preserves a valid zero. The five-character string key preserves leading zeros. Census results match the saved code by text, not by geocoding; [a ZCTA is not a postal ZIP](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/geography/guidance/geo-areas/zctas.html). Missing observations are never substituted from a nearby ZIP or county.

The compact snapshot tuple is `[[gross rent, MOE] or null, [home value, MOE] or null, [HPI observation year, annual change in basis points] or null]`. When updating, review the new release and hash, update the dates and labels together, regenerate in a reviewed PR, then run the 390 px Market Comparison browser smoke. There is no automatic provider refresh.

## Reading and comparing observations

The staff panel counts which of the three published measures are available. Census details show each metric's definition, published 90% margin of error, and interval (estimate plus or minus that margin, with the lower bound limited to zero). The margin as a percentage of the estimate is descriptive, not a Census quality grade. For example, ZCTA 91401 monthly gross rent is $1,881 with a $43 margin: the displayed range is $1,838 to $1,924.

The optional second-ZIP lookup uses the bundled data only. In each Census comparison, both rows use one zero-based scale, but rent and home value always use separate scales. Interval overlap is not a [statistical significance test](https://www.census.gov/help/topics/faq.do-i-need-to-consider-the-margins-of-error-when-comparing-acs-estimates.html). FHFA observations retain their actual years and are not compared as if they were for one year when vintages differ. Neither a survey interval nor a historical index change estimates what the saved house is worth or how it will perform.

## Staff-only ZIP 91411 pilot

The `codex/house-zip-91411-pilot` Deploy Preview adds two public snapshots. Both were checked November 23, 2026. Netlify team sign-in protects the preview. The pilot chunk and QA entry are excluded from production builds, and the pilot panel only renders for a saved postal ZIP of 91411.

- **HUD FY 2027.** The [Los Angeles County Small Area FMR table](https://www.huduser.gov/datasets/fmr/fmr2027/summary?mode=safmr&year=2027&reporttype=county&states=6&counties=0603799999) reports 91411 monthly gross rent benchmarks of $2,360, $2,630, $3,250, $4,120, and $4,610 for studio through four bedrooms. Use the county lookup to verify the row before refreshing.
- **City of Los Angeles building permits.** The [LADBS dataset](https://data.lacity.org/City-Infrastructure-Service-Requests/Building-and-Safety-Building-Permits-Issued-from-2/pi9x-tg5x) returned 36 unique October 2026 building permits in postal ZIP 91411 for one- and two-family dwellings. Counts by type were one new construction, six additions, and 29 alterations or repairs. The figures cover permits issued within the city's jurisdiction.
- **Closed sales and signed leases.** The pilot omits transaction amounts and has no provider connector. Reventure remains the [research hub](https://blog.reventure.app/graph). The fictional trend chart retains its label and preview restriction.

To reproduce the permit snapshot, query the public `pi9x-tg5x` resource with `zip_code = '91411'`, `issue_date >= '2026-10-01T00:00:00'`, `issue_date < '2026-11-01T00:00:00'`, and `permit_sub_type = '1 or 2 Family Dwelling'`. Count distinct `permit_nbr` by `permit_type`, retaining the `Bldg-New`, `Bldg-Addition`, and `Bldg-Alter/Repair` categories. Display aggregate counts only. These dated snapshots refresh through a reviewed code change.
